student receives a question, enters the Board, thinks through theproblem, writes/types/speaks/draws their reasoning, and the AI observestheir work and guides them toward understanding.The AI should help the student THINK rather than simply provide the answer.Core loop:QUESTION↓AI UNDERSTANDS↓BOARD OPENS↓AI INTRODUCES THE QUESTION↓AI DECOMPOSES THE QUESTION↓STUDENT ATTEMPTS↓AI OBSERVES↓AI DETECTS ERRORS / MISSING CONCEPTS↓AI HINTS / GUIDES↓STUDENT CORRECTS↓AI VALIDATES↓NEXT STEP↓FINAL UNDERSTANDING↓FINAL REPORT↓LEARNING JOURNEY UPDATE↓NEXT LEARNING RECOMMENDATION---# FUNDAMENTAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLEThe student does the thinking.Stepwise should NOT immediately solve the student's question.It should guide the student through the reasoning process.The AI should:- understand student language- understand imperfect grammar- understand partial answers- detect conceptual errors- identify missing information- provide appropriate hints- intervene when necessary- adapt explanations- use visuals when helpful- gradually increase support- gradually reduce support as the student becomes independent---# THE BOARDThe Board is the central workspace of Stepwise.Students should be able to:- type- write- draw- speak- move text- move visual objects- annotate visuals- connect ideas- correct their work- ask AI to analyze the Board- save the Board- reopen the Board- export the Board as an imageAI-generated diagrams/cards/visuals should generally be movable andannotatable without allowing the student to accidentally modify theoriginal generated visual content.---# QUESTION TYPESStepwise must NOT be limited to mathematics.It should support:- mathematics- physics- chemistry- biology- computer science- history- geography- languages- theoretical concepts- definitions- essays- explanations- reasoning questions- conceptual questions- numerical problems- multi-step problems- open-ended questionsThe AI should determine the appropriate teaching strategy based on thequestion.---# STUDENT LANGUAGEStudents do NOT need to match a perfect textbook answer.The AI evaluates meaning and understanding.For example:Student:"photosynthesis is when plants make food using sun"The AI should understand the intended meaning even though the sentenceis informal.It should distinguish between:- correct meaning- incomplete meaning- incorrect meaning- poor wording- grammar issuesGrammar should NOT automatically be treated as conceptual error.---# QUESTION DECOMPOSITIONThe AI can divide a question into appropriate steps.For example:1. Identify the concept.2. Identify the relevant information.3. Apply the concept.4. Check the reasoning.5. Produce the final answer.The number of steps should NOT be fixed.Some questions may need:2 stepsOthers:4 stepsOthers:6+ stepsThe decomposition must depend on the actual question.---# ERROR SYSTEMWhen the student makes a mistake:The Board should visually identify the relevant part.Possible indicators:- red highlight- circle- label- arrow- explanation cardThe AI should explain:- what is wrong- why it is wrong- what is missing- what the student should reconsiderDo not automatically replace the student's work with the correct answer.---# HINT SYSTEMHints should escalate.LEVEL 1:Small directional hint.LEVEL 2:More specific conceptual hint.LEVEL 3:Example or visual support.LEVEL 4:More explicit guidance.LEVEL 5:Detailed explanation when necessary.The goal is to use the smallest useful hint.---# VISUAL LEARNINGWhen useful, Stepwise can generate:- diagrams- cards- graphs- mind maps- concept maps- timelines- process diagrams- labeled illustrations- mathematical visualsVisuals should support understanding rather than exist purely for decoration.---# ANALYZE BOARDThe student can explicitly ask:"Analyze my Board."The AI should evaluate the current Board and explain:- what is correct- what is partially correct- what is incorrect- what is missing- what needs improvement- what the student should do nextIt should not simply rewrite the student's answer.---# LEARNING JOURNEYEvery completed learning session contributes evidence to the student'slong-term learning journey.Track concepts such as:- understanding- mastery- misconceptions- prerequisites- repeated errors- self-corrections- hint dependency- recent practice- recommended reviewFocus on the CURRENT topic while maintaining long-term context.---# FINAL REPORTAt the end of a session, Stepwise should provide:- time spent- steps completed- errors- corrections- concepts understood- concepts that remain weak- important misconceptions- detailed explanation of the question- final understanding- recommended next learning- relevant prerequisites- learning progression---# ADAPTIVE UIStepwise is ONE product.Do NOT create completely separate Kids and University products.The interface should adapt to:- age- knowledge level- subject- difficulty- cognitive load- language- accessibility- learning preferences- autonomy levelA young learner should receive a simple friendly interface.A university student should receive a powerful academic workspace.The underlying learning system remains the same.---# ARCHITECTURE PRINCIPLESBuild Stepwise as a modular application.Major systems include:- Authentication- User profiles- Question system- Session system- Board system- AI Brain- Teaching Engine- Visual Learning Engine- Learning Journey- Final Reports- Mastery system- Misconception system- Prerequisite system- Saved Boards- Settings- Accessibility- Security- AnalyticsKeep systems modular and maintainable.---# AI ARCHITECTUREDo not hard-code the application to one AI provider.Create an AI abstraction layer so models/providers can be changed later.AI functions should include concepts such as:- analyzeQuestion- decomposeQuestion- analyzeBoard- evaluateStudentWork- generateHint- generateVisual- generateExplanation- generateFinalReport- updateLearningJourney- recommendNextLearning- detectMisconceptionAI output must be structured and validated before it changes applicationstate.The AI must NEVER directly execute:- SQL- shell commands- arbitrary code- database commands- filesystem commandsAI produces structured data.Application code validates and processes that data.---# SECURITYSecurity is a core requirement.Never expose API keys in frontend code.Use secure authentication.Verify authorization for every protected resource.Users must only access their own:- sessions- Boards- reports- learning history- uploaded filesProtect against:- SQL injection- XSS- CSRF where applicable- IDOR- session attacks- unsafe file uploads- prompt injection- malicious AI output- rate-limit abuseTreat all student content as untrusted input.---# PERFORMANCEThe Board must feel immediate.Do not send an AI request for every:- keystroke- mouse movement- object movementOnly perform AI analysis when meaningful events occur.Use:- local state- optimistic updates- debounced persistence- caching- lazy loading- code splitting- efficient rendering- pagination- background processing where appropriateThe application must work well on low-end/mobile devices as well asdesktop computers.---# RESPONSIVE DESIGNSupport:- mobile- tablet- laptop- desktopDo not simply shrink the desktop UI.The Board must remain the primary workspace on every screen size.---# ACCESSIBILITYSupport:- keyboard navigation- screen readers- scalable text- high contrast- reduced motion- captions- voice interaction- large touch targets- non-color error indicators---# DATA OWNERSHIPThe student's work belongs to the student's learning experience.Never silently erase or replace their work.Preserve useful evidence of their thinking and corrections.Autosave the Board.Provide recovery when possible.---# IMPLEMENTATION RULEIMPORTANT:DO NOT attempt to build the entire Stepwise system in one giant generationstep.First understand the entire specification.Then build incrementally.The application must remain functional after every major phase.---# IMPLEMENTATION PHASES## PHASE 1 — Student signs in.2. Student enters a question.3. Board opens.4. AI introduces the question.5. AI tells the student what to do first.6. Student writes/types an attempt.7. AI analyzes the attempt.8. AI highlights errors or missing concepts.9. AI gives a hint.10. Student corrects the work.11. AI validates it.12. AI moves to the next step.13. Student reaches the final understanding.14. Final Report is generated.15. Learning Journey is updated.Do not prioritize decorative features over this loop.---# IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT BEHAVIORBefore modifying architecture:Read all files in /docs.Before implementing a feature:Check whether that feature is already specified.Do not contradict the specifications.If two specifications appear to conflict:Prefer the more recent detailed engineering requirement while preservingthe core product philosophy.If something is technically impossible or ambiguous:Explain the issue and propose the safest implementation instead of silentlychanging the product concept.Do not remove major functionality simply because it is difficult.Do not create fake functionality and present it as working functionality.If an external service is not connected, clearly isolate it and use adevelopment mock only where appropriate.---# MOST IMPORTANT RULESTEPWISE IS NOT A CHATBOT WITH A WHITEBOARD.STEPWISE IS AN INTERACTIVE AI LEARNING ENVIRONMENT.THE BOARD IS THE CLASSROOM.THE AI IS THE TEACHER.THE STUDENT'S WORK IS THE EVIDENCE.THE TEACHING ENGINE IS THE GUIDANCE SYSTEM.THE VISUAL ENGINE IS THE EXPLANATION LAYER.THE LEARNING JOURNEY IS THE LONG-TERM MEMORY.THE FINAL REPORT IS THE REFLECTION.THE RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM IS THE NEXT PATH.THE STUDENT DOES THE THINKING.BUILD EVERYTHING AROUND THAT PRINCIPLE.
